The Heat

Authentic Heritage

Unlike modern spas, we preserve the traditional Hanjeungmak—a stone and yellow clay kiln heated by burning pine trees. The deep infrared heat restores and detoxifies.

Sauna Etiquette Guide
The Main Kiln

Traditional Hanjeungmak

Built with stone and yellow clay (황토), this kiln is the soul of our spa. It emits far-infrared rays that penetrate deep to boost circulation and metabolism.

  • Extreme HeatCeiling temperatures exceed 100°C, while breathing level is over 80°C.
  • Safe PacingRecommended stay is 3–5 minutes per session. No clocks are placed inside for your safety.
  • Rest & HydrateAlways follow each session with 5+ minutes of rest in the lounge.
Themed Rooms

A variety of warmth

Salt Sauna

50°C – 80°C · 10–15 min

Warm mineral salt room for skin health and joint relief. If sweating heavily, please sit rather than lie down to protect the salt floor.

Amethyst Sauna

Calming Relaxation

Crystal-infused thermal room designed for gentle heat and mental tranquility. Ideal for longer, meditative sessions.

Red Clay (Hwangto)

Gentle Far-Infrared

Earthy clay sauna providing a natural, steady warmth that helps detoxify the skin and soothe tired muscles.
